# LOB Alpha Simulator & Market-Making Engine

## Executive Summary
An event-driven limit order book (LOB) simulator engineered in Python to evaluate microstructure alpha signals and passive quoting strategies under realistic execution frictions.

## Key Microstructure Signals
- **Order Book Imbalance (OBI):** Normalized difference between top bid and ask depth.
- **Volume-Weighted Micro-Price:** Queue-weighted fair price estimator.
- **Spread Dynamics:** Liquidity exhaustion metrics.

## The Robustness Story
Naive backtesting at mid-price produced unrealistic Sharpe ratios. After incorporating full bid-ask crossing and exchange taker fees, the edge evaporated. The model was pivoted to passive market making with adverse-selection penalty modeling: ~35% fill rate on winning quotes, 100% fill rate on toxic quotes, with a >60% probability threshold for directional skew.
