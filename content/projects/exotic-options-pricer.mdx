# Exotic Options Pricing Engine + Web Client

## Executive Summary
A vectorized Monte Carlo valuation kernel pricing path-dependent derivatives under Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM), paired with closed-form Black-Scholes control variates and central difference Greeks.

## Architecture
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python 3.11, pure NumPy vectorization across 100,000 diffusion trajectories.
- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Recharts, and interactive parameter exploration.

## Variance Reduction
Antithetic variates and closed-form European call control variates reduce standard error significantly compared to crude Monte Carlo at identical path counts.
