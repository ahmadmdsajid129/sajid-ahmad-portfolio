# HimaVogue: Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform & Atelier

## Executive Summary
Full-stack monolithic marketplace built on Next.js 14 (App Router) and MongoDB, featuring a multi-vendor escrow ledger, split-order logistics, server-verified eSewa payments, and a bespoke tailoring quotation state machine.

## Key Engineering Modules
1. **Authentication:** Short-lived access token + long-lived bcrypt-hashed refresh token in MongoDB; silent refresh request queue preventing redundant token issuance.
2. **Checkout Integrity:** 100% server-side price recalculation from MongoDB catalog; direct server-to-server validation of eSewa transaction receipts.
3. **Split Orders:** Parent order with pro-rated discounts and coupon distribution across independent vendor child shipments.
4. **Inventory Concurrency:** Atomic `$inc` on `variants.stockBySize` guarded by `$gte` balance filters to eliminate overselling under concurrent checkouts.
5. **Wallet & Rewards Ledger:** Expiring point batches sorted by FIFO maturity; immediate deduction at draft creation with auditable mutation history.
6. **Multi-Vendor Escrow:** Vendor balances held in `pendingClearance` until customer delivery and return period expiration.
7. **Atelier State Machine:** Tailoring quotation negotiation with 48-hour fit alteration tracking.
8. **Caching & Search:** Redis cache-aside with glob invalidation and graceful fallback; MiniSearch in-memory autocomplete.
9. **Operations & Cron:** Scheduled cleanup of abandoned drafts, coupon distribution, and velocity scoring.
10. **Headless Admin CMS:** Dynamic banner layouts, promotional campaigns, and interactive scratch card gamification.

## Part B: Quant Lens on a Real Business
- **Critique of Heuristic:** The static velocity score formula (`3*sales + 2*cart_adds + 1*views`) uses unnormalized arbitrary coefficients with cliff-edge epoch resets.
- **Planned Quantitative Enhancements:** Walk-forward demand forecasting, Bayesian Thompson-sampling velocity ranking, and newsvendor inventory safety stock optimization.
