# Real-Time Payment Fraud Detection & Risk Engine

## Executive Summary
A streaming fraud-scoring pipeline combining calibrated XGBoost, Isolation Forest anomaly scoring, Redis sliding-window features, Kafka pub/sub, and TreeSHAP explainability.

## Benchmark Performance
Evaluated on a held-out chronological test slice of 2,250 transactions (2.25% fraud prevalence):
- **Champion XGBoost:** PR-AUC 0.9825, ROC-AUC 0.9990, F1 0.9431, Recall 0.9508, Precision 0.9355, Brier 0.0023.
- **Isotonic Calibration:** Improves Brier score to 0.0018 with Expected Calibration Error (ECE) of 0.0014.
- **Latency Benchmark:** Measured total HTTP round trip p95 of 92.24ms (under 100ms SLA).
